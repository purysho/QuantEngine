            Market data (Alpaca)

            NOT CONNECTED

            Live US stock and ETF prices, news and option chains. Create a free account at alpaca.markets, open the paper trading dashboard and generate API keys. Paper keys cannot move real money. QuantEngine reads market data and, only if you enable paper trading on the Strategies page and approve each list, places orders in your Alpaca paper (simulated) account. It cannot reach Alpaca's live trading host at all. Keys are encrypted by Windows and never shown again. The free IEX feed is real-time but covers about 2.5% of US volume, so its volume figures are partial.
